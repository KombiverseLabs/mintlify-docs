<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Go to [workbench.kombify.io](https://workbench.kombify.io) and sign in with your kombify account. Authentication uses the standard kombify sign-in (Auth0) — the same account you use for the [kombify Cloud dashboard](https://app.kombify.io).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your plan">
    AI features are enabled per subscription plan. If the Workbench reports no available models or features, your current plan does not include them yet — see your plan details in the [Cloud dashboard](https://app.kombify.io) or the [pricing overview](/cloud/pricing).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a conversation">
    Open a new thread and ask something. Responses stream in realtime; your threads are stored as sessions you can return to.
  </Step>
</Steps>
