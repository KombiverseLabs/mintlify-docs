The **Floating Panel** is kombify's embeddable AI surface: a compact assistant panel that kombify tools integrate so you can use kombify AI without leaving the tool you are working in.

## Where you meet it

kombify products embed the panel directly — look for the AI action in the tool's interface. The panel is served by the AI Platform (`/v1/embed/floating-panel`) and talks to the same API as the [Workbench](/ai/workbench), so:

- You sign in once with your kombify account — the panel reuses the platform session.
- Conversations are platform sessions; a thread started in a tool can be continued in the Workbench.
- Plan entitlements apply consistently across every surface.

## For your own products

The Floating Panel is built as an embeddable component, and embedding it into your own applications is part of the kombify AI roadmap for platform customers. If you want to integrate kombify AI into your product today, the supported path is the [platform API](/ai/reference/api).

<Note>
  Details of the embed contract are still stabilizing during beta — this page will grow an integration guide as the public embed path is finalized.
</Note>
