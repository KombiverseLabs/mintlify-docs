The public Guide is the anonymous, tool-free AI entry surface.

## Target capability contract

- answer with curated public knowledge boundaries,
- include source citations where available,
- route users to docs, OSS install paths, pricing, or signup.

## What the Guide cannot do

- call product tools,
- access customer data,
- perform account-scoped operations,
- claim SaaS availability from local/self-host proof.

Guide experiences can appear on `kombify.io`, `ai.kombify.io`, and approved
first-party embeds while preserving this boundary.

<Warning>
Availability/evidence: this page defines the public Guide contract. It is not a
live-SaaS availability claim by itself and must not be used as runtime proof.
</Warning>
