The public Guide is the anonymous, tool-free AI entry surface.

## What the Guide can do

- answer using curated public knowledge,
- include source citations,
- point users to docs, OSS install paths, pricing, or signup.

## What the Guide cannot do

- call product tools,
- access customer data,
- perform account-scoped operations,
- claim SaaS availability from local/self-host proof.

Guide experiences can appear on `kombify.io`, `ai.kombify.io`, and approved
first-party embeds while preserving this boundary.
