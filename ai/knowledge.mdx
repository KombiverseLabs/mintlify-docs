kombify AI can answer questions **grounded in kombify's knowledge base** — documentation, product knowledge, and guides — instead of relying on model memory alone.

## How it works

Knowledge queries run retrieval-augmented generation on the platform: your question is matched against indexed kombify content, and the model answers from the retrieved passages. This powers AI answers about kombify products across the platform surfaces.

- Available through the platform API at `/v1/ai/knowledge/query`.
- Answers cite the knowledge they used, so you can verify against the source page.
- The index covers public kombify content; what a query can see never exceeds what your account may access.

## Asking good knowledge questions

- Ask about **kombify products and workflows** ("How do I add the backup add-on to a Basement Kit?") — that is what the index covers.
- For general-purpose questions, use a normal [Workbench](/ai/workbench) thread instead; knowledge queries are scoped on purpose.

<Note>
  Knowledge coverage grows continuously as documentation and product knowledge are indexed. If an answer seems incomplete, check the linked source pages — the documentation is always the authority.
</Note>
