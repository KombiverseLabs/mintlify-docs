Kombify Companion is the native mobile surface for:

- authenticated AI/support access,
- read-only runtime status and evidence views,
- approval-state and action-card rendering,
- notifications and deep-link evidence flow.

Companion is intentionally curated and does not replace full desktop/web admin
surfaces or durable orchestration authority.

<Warning>
Availability/evidence: approve/deny/resume side effects and SpeechKit voice UI
are target/preview capabilities and not current Mobile implemented capability.
</Warning>
