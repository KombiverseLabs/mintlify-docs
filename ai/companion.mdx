Kombify Companion is the native mobile surface for:

- authenticated AI/support access,
- runtime status and alerts,
- approve/deny action cards,
- notifications and deep-link evidence flow.

Companion is intentionally curated and does not replace full desktop/web admin
surfaces or durable orchestration authority.
