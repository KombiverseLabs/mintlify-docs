SpeechKit is the voice framework used across Companion, Workbench, and approved
embedded surfaces.

## Core rules

- raw microphone audio is local/LAN by default,
- hosted voice requires explicit entitlement and user choice,
- MCP and product APIs expose voice diagnostics/management, not raw audio
  streams,
- denials must include clear reason and user guidance before side effects.

SpeechKit voice preview is included in the premium beta scope; local homelab
voice remains first-class and does not require hosted SaaS.
