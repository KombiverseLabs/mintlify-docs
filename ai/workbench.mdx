Kombify Workbench is the full authenticated web product for:

- AI conversations and support,
- agent operations and proposal workflows,
- session/evidence visibility,
- traces and operator-grade run insight.

Workbench is a product surface, not policy authority. Identity, entitlement,
quota, and MCP policy remain governed by the central gateway + identity stack.
