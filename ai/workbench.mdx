The **Workbench** at [workbench.kombify.io](https://workbench.kombify.io) is the web surface for kombify AI: a focused chat experience backed by the same AI Platform API that powers every other kombify AI surface.

## What you get

- **Streaming threads** — responses render as they are generated.
- **Session history** — conversations are stored as sessions on the platform; pick any thread up where you left it, from any device.
- **Model transparency** — each response is tied to a model execution you can inspect (which model ran, token usage).

## Signing in

The Workbench uses your kombify account (Auth0 sign-in). Access and available capability depend on your subscription plan — the Workbench shows what your plan currently enables.

## Threads and sessions

A thread in the Workbench is a **session** on the AI Platform. That means:

- The same session data is accessible via the [platform API](/ai/reference/api) (`/v1/ai/sessions`), so your own tooling can work with conversations started in the Workbench.
- Deleting a session removes it from your history.

<Note>
  The Workbench is in active beta — additional capabilities (for example document workspaces) are rolling out gradually and appear automatically when they are enabled for your plan.
</Note>
