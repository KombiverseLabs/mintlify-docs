Every kombify.me address points somewhere. There are three kinds. Pick the one
that matches where your app lives.

## The three kinds

| Kind | In everyday terms | Use it when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public address (`proxy`) | kombify.me stands in front of your app and passes visitors through. The visitor still sees the kombify.me name. | Your app can already be reached from the internet. |
| Redirect | kombify.me sends the visitor on to another web address. The browser then shows that other address. | You only want a short, memorable name that forwards elsewhere. |
| Private address (`tunnel`) | A small helper at your home opens a connection out to kombify.me and carries requests in. Nothing needs to be opened on your router. | Your app is only reachable inside your home network. |

## How to choose

1. Can someone outside your home open the app's web address today? If yes,
   choose a **public address**.
2. Do you just want a nicer name for a page that already lives elsewhere?
   Choose a **redirect**.
3. Is the app only reachable at addresses like `192.168.x.x` or `localhost`?
   Choose a **private address**.

A public address never accepts a private, loopback or link-local address. This
protects your home network. If you try, you get an error and nothing is saved.

## What a private address needs

- A Techstack node agent running at home that can reach the app.
- The agent must stay connected. If it is disconnected, visitors get a "try again
  in a few seconds" answer (503).
- Pages and files are limited to 10 MiB per request or response and 30 seconds per
  request. Live streams and arbitrary network connections are not supported.

See [Reach a private service](/kombify-me/private-targets) for the full path.

## Public does not mean "everyone must see it"

A public address can be opened by anyone who knows it, unless your app asks for
a login. The app keeps its own sign-in and cookies. Keep apps with sensitive
data behind their own login, or leave them off until you are ready.

Every app also starts switched off. Nobody can open it until you switch it on.

kombify.me can also share an app for a limited time with named visitors. That
is a separate feature with its own owner controls in the kombify dashboard. It
is not covered here.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Error saving a private address on a public address | The public path refuses private addresses. Use a private address instead. |
| 503 "try again" on a private address | The home helper is not connected. Start it and wait a moment. |
| 502 "Bad Gateway" on a public address | kombify.me could not reach your app. Check that it runs and is public. |

Hosted `*.lan.kombify.me` names are not implemented. StackKits provides names
inside your home network.
