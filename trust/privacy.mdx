there is a difference, the legal text applies.

## Your homelab

- Data of services on your server, such as photos and documents, stays on your
  hardware.
- A connected server talks to the control service over authenticated,
  encrypted connections. The agent does not send the content of your services.
- A self-managed installation sends its operating data only to your own
  Techstack.
- Looking at content, for example photo previews in an app, would be an
  opt-in per use case and off by default. This is a design that is still being
  built and is not available today.

## Where hosted data is stored

According to the privacy policy, kombify's applications run in Frankfurt am
Main, the databases in Frankfurt, and files and backups in the EU region of the
storage provider. The public API runs on a worldwide network. Some providers
are companies outside the EU. The legal basis for those transfers and the
complete list of service providers are in the
[privacy policy](https://kombify.io/privacy#subprocessors). Customers under the
data processing agreement are told at least 30 days before a provider changes.

## What is collected

- **Account:** the data you give when you register, and billing data from the
  payment provider.
- **Technical logs:** short-lived server logs.
- **Analytics:** only with your consent and only the pseudonymous usage data
  described in the policy. Without consent the analytics tool is not loaded. No
  advertising cookies and no cross-site tracking.
- **Error monitoring:** without cookies, form contents or session recording.
- **Audio:** kombify does not store your voice. It is processed and passed on
  as described below.

Your choices about cookies are in the "Cookie settings" link in the footer of
kombify.io. See also the [cookie section of the policy](https://kombify.io/privacy#cookies).

## AI and your content

- **kombify does not use your content to train AI models.**
- AI inputs and answers are not written to kombify's logs.
- Model providers that process a request may keep inputs and outputs for a
  limited time, for example to detect misuse. We do not promise that a
  provider deletes everything immediately.
- Conversation history and memories are stored in your account until you
  delete them or your account.
- A provider outside the EU, such as DeepSeek, is never used automatically. It
  needs a separate notice and your explicit consent, and you can withdraw it by
  choosing another model.
- The interface tells you when you talk to an AI.
- kombify makes no automated decisions with legal effect about you.

### Voice

The hosted voice service is used only after your express consent. Where your
device supports it, speech is processed on the device. With SpeechKit you decide
where processing happens. See [Choose your privacy settings](/speechkit/privacy-settings).

## Delete your data

- **Delete your account:** write to [info@kombify.io](mailto:info@kombify.io)
  from your account settings or by e-mail. Account data is deleted within 30
  days of the request. Your identity may be checked first.
- **Paid plans:** when a contract ends you have 30 days to export your data.
  After that, servers, storage and backups operated by kombify are deleted. You
  get an e-mail beforehand.
- **Your own hardware:** what runs on your own computers is yours and is not
  deleted by kombify.
- **Backups** are overwritten in the normal rotation.
- **Billing records** must be kept for the periods required by law. Invoices
  are kept for 8 years.

## Your rights

You can ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction, a copy of your data
and you can object to processing. You can complain to the supervisory
authority responsible for kombify, the data protection authority of
North Rhine-Westphalia. Write to [info@kombify.io](mailto:info@kombify.io).
