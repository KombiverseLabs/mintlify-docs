This section explains in plain words how kombify treats your data and your
systems. It describes what is true today. Where something is planned, it says
so, and it never presents a plan as a feature.

<Info>
The legal texts are binding and take precedence over this page:
[Privacy policy](https://kombify.io/privacy) and
[Terms](https://kombify.io/terms), which include the data processing
agreement. Contact: [info@kombify.io](mailto:info@kombify.io).
</Info>

## The short version

- **You choose where your data lives.** Software you run yourself, such as
  StackKits, keeps its data on your own computers and works without a kombify
  account.
- **Connecting is a choice.** A connection to a kombify service adds sign-in or
  monitoring. Photos and documents on your server are not sent to kombify.
- **Secrets stay secret.** Keys and tokens are kept in secret stores, not in
  source code, and are not written into logs.
- **Nobody gets more power than you.** An assistant that acts for you never has
  more authority than you.
- **We do not train AI on your content.**
- **Everything is in beta.** There is no service-level agreement yet, including
  on paid plans.

## What this section does not claim

kombify has no external penetration test and no security certification of its
own. Testing is done internally. See [Security](/trust/security#how-we-test).
