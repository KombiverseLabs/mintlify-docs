TinyAuth is the default forward-auth entrypoint in the released StackKits catalog. It normally appears at `auth.<domain>` and uses the local PocketID owner setup for protected routes.

After a successful rollout, open the generated address, complete the local owner setup, and verify that a protected route redirects through the expected login path.

If a route loops or bypasses authentication, inspect the rollout before changing an application:

```bash
stackkit verify --json
stackkit status --json
```

See [PocketID](/stackkits/apps/pocketid) for the local identity component.
