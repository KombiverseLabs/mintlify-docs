sidebarTitle: "Whoami"

---

Whoami ships as a v0.16.0 routing-diagnostic module contract, but the initial native v2 StackSpec does not select it. Do not expect a generated Whoami route in a fresh Basement Kit or copy an address from an older rollout.

Do not change generated router configuration in response to one diagnostic request. Update validated StackKits intent and regenerate the rollout instead.
