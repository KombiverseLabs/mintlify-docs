Vaultwarden is the default password-vault application in the released StackKits catalog. When it is enabled, use the generated `vault.<domain>` address only after the StackKits rollout has verified successfully.

Create the application owner account, store recovery material separately, and choose the registration policy before adding real secrets. Confirm that your backup and recovery approach covers the Vaultwarden data volume.

Change StackKits integration through the StackSpec and lifecycle commands; do not edit generated application configuration on the target.
