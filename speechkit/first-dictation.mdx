This guide takes you from nothing to your first spoken sentence turned into
text. It assumes Windows 10 or 11 and no previous experience.

## Before you start

- A Windows 10 or 11 computer with a 64-bit processor (x64).
- A working microphone.

SpeechKit is beta software, and the free Windows release is unsigned.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download and check the installer">
    Follow [Install on Windows](/speechkit/install-windows). Download only from
    the official release, compare the checksum and then start the installer.
    Windows may show a reputation warning because the release is unsigned.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start SpeechKit and choose your microphone">
    On the first start, select the microphone that you want to use.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your privacy setting">
    Pick a Network Scope. If you do not want your voice to leave the computer,
    choose a local provider and a stricter scope. The page
    [Choose your privacy settings](/speechkit/privacy-settings) explains the
    choices.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test Dictation first">
    Click into a text field in any app, for example a note. Use the default
    Dictation hotkey, `Ctrl+Win`, and speak a sentence.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Look at the result">
    The text appears in the field where your cursor was.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it worked

Your spoken sentence appears as text. If not, see the section below.

## If nothing happens

- Check that the right microphone is selected.
- Check that the provider you chose is available. A local provider needs to be
  installed on the computer.
- A stricter Network Scope suspends settings that need the internet. Settings
  tell you what is disabled and why.
- Try Dictation before Assist, Voice Agent or Meeting.
