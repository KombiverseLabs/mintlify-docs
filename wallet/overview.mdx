<Warning>
The kombify Wallet is not released yet. These pages describe how it works so
you know what to expect. You cannot use it today.
</Warning>

The kombify Wallet is the one place where kombify keeps what you hand it: API
keys, logins, variables, configuration files, and the ways you reach your
servers. You, your agents and the kombify products you choose use these items
in the same way, and each of them gets only the rights you give it.

## What it holds

| Item | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **API key, token or certificate** | One secret value, such as a provider's API key. |
| **Login** | A username and password for another service, with an optional one-time code secret. |
| **Variable** | One value your tools look up by name, such as `DATABASE_URL`. |
| **Configuration** | A document, such as an `.env` file. |
| **Server access** | How to reach a server: host, user, and an SSH private key or a password. |
| **SSH certificate authority** | Signs short-lived SSH certificates for you, your agents and your servers. |

Accounts you connect to other services, for example through OAuth, are
planned and will arrive with their own flow.

## How the Wallet protects what you keep

- **Encrypted before it leaves your device.** Your browser, or the
  `kombify-wallet` command line, encrypts a value on your device. The services
  between you and the Wallet only relay encrypted data, and the Wallet stores
  only encrypted values. The keys that open them are held apart from the
  stored data, so a copy of the stored data alone cannot be read.
- **Every use is recorded.** The Wallet writes an audit record before it
  releases or uses a value, whether you reveal it, an agent uses it or a
  product uses it. If it cannot write the record, it does not release the
  value.
- **Your Auth0 sign-in guards access.** You reach your Wallet by signing in to
  your kombify account through Auth0 at `login.kombify.io`. There is no second
  Wallet password. Only you, signed in yourself, can manage your Wallet.
  Agents and delegated sessions cannot.
- **You set the rights, one by one.** Using an item, reading its value and
  managing it are separate rights. An agent or a product never inherits
  yours.

<Note>
Anyone who can sign in to your kombify account can open your Wallet as you.
Protect the account with a passkey or multi-factor sign-in.
</Note>

## What the Wallet cannot do

A value that someone has already seen cannot be taken back. If a value may
have reached someone it should not have, add a new version of the item to
replace it.
