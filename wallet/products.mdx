<Note>
The kombify Wallet is not released yet. This page describes how it works.
</Note>

Some kombify products work for you while you are away: they sync your mail,
call a provider or run your servers. When a product needs a key or a login
from your Wallet, you assign that item to it. A product never gets your other
items.

## Assign an item to a product

1. Open **Settings** and choose **Keys & connections**.
2. In the **Wallet** section, select **Products** on the item.
3. Switch on the rights you want for each product.

The three rights are the same as for [agents](/wallet/agents): **Use**,
**Read** and **Manage**. Each is granted on its own.

| Product | What it does for you |
| --- | --- |
| kombify Techstack | Plans and runs your servers and stacks. |
| Paperwork | Handles your mail, documents and tasks. |
| kombify AI Platform | Runs AI features and agents for you. |
| kombify Simulate | Runs simulations of your setups. |
| kombify Cloud | Handles your kombify Cloud account features. |

## What a product can and cannot do

- A product uses only the items you assigned to it. It acts on your standing
  assignment, so you do not need to be signed in.
- With **Use**, a product makes calls with the item, asks an SSH certificate
  authority for certificates, or receives the value for a single use that its
  work needs, such as syncing mail.
- A product never receives a use-only value. It can only make calls with it
  through its [use targets](/wallet/use-only-values).
- A product never receives the key of an SSH certificate authority.
- Give **Manage** only for an item the product keeps up to date itself, such as
  a connection whose token the provider rotates. It lets the product store new
  versions of that item.
- Every use is recorded, as for you and your agents.

## Let a product add items

Some products can move a secret you already keep into your Wallet. Select
**Products that add items** above the list, then switch on **May add items**
for each product you allow. An item a product adds is assigned to that product
so it can use it. The product still uses only the items you assign to it.

## Take access away

Switch the right off. It applies from the product's next operation, and an
operation that was already allowed lasts about a minute at most. If the product
has received an item's value, add a new version of the item afterwards. The
Wallet does not yet mark the item for replacement for you.
