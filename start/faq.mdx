## About kombify

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is kombify finished?">
    No. The documentation says on every page how far a product has come.
    StackKits and SpeechKit have public releases, SpeechKit is a beta, and
    Techstack, kombify.me and Companion are earlier. kombify Cloud is in a
    public beta, where availability is not guaranteed. See
    [Which product do I need?](/start/choose-your-product).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a kombify account?">
    Not for StackKits or SpeechKit. You need one for kombify Cloud and for the
    studio that belongs to the AI tools.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do I get help?">
    Write to [info@kombify.io](mailto:info@kombify.io). Name the product, what
    you did and the time it happened. The current service status is at
    [status.kombify.io](https://status.kombify.io).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Homelab

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What hardware do I need?">
    A computer you control that can stay on. The supported operating systems
    and the evidence behind them are listed in
    [OS compatibility](/stackkits/reference/os-compatibility). Read
    [Before you start](/guides/stackkits/before-you-start) first.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does kombify see my photos and files?">
    StackKits runs on your own computer and works without a kombify account.
    Your data stays on the machines you operate unless you decide to publish a
    service or to connect a hosted service yourself. See
    [Privacy and data](/trust/privacy).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use it only inside my home network?">
    Yes. A Basement Kit is built for that. Reaching it from outside is an extra
    step you choose. See [Use your homelab day to day](/guides/stackkits/daily-use).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if I lose access to my homelab?">
    Keep the access details created during setup somewhere private, and set up
    and practice a restore. See [Back up and restore](/guides/stackkits/back-up-and-restore)
    and [Troubleshooting](/guides/stackkits/troubleshooting).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Voice

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does SpeechKit send my voice to the internet?">
    Only if you choose an external provider. A local provider keeps the work on
    your device. See [Choose your privacy settings](/speechkit/privacy-settings).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Windows warns me when I install SpeechKit. Is that normal?">
    The free Windows release is unsigned, so Windows may show a reputation
    warning. Check the checksum and the release origin as described in
    [Install on Windows](/speechkit/install-windows).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Account and billing

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I cancel?">
    Open billing in the dashboard, choose **Manage subscription** and cancel in
    the billing portal. Your plan runs until the end of the period you paid
    for. See [Change or cancel your plan](/apps/cloud/plans-and-billing).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I cannot sign in.">
    See [Sign-in problems](/apps/cloud/sign-in-and-account).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
