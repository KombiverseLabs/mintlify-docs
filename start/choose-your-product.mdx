kombify is several products that work on their own and fit together. You do
not need all of them. Find the sentence that matches what you want, then open
the page next to it.

## Start from your goal

| I want to... | Use | Where to begin |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Run my own photo library, file storage, password vault or media server at home | StackKits | [Before you start](/guides/stackkits/before-you-start), then the [Quickstart](/stackkits/quickstart) |
| Have a guided screen for setting up and looking after my servers | Techstack (Alpha) | [Techstack overview](/techstack/overview) |
| Reach a service at home from outside with a normal `https://` address | kombify.me (Alpha) | [kombify.me overview](/kombify-me/overview) |
| Type or talk to my computer instead of using the keyboard | SpeechKit | [Your first dictation](/speechkit/first-dictation) |
| Have a kombify account, a dashboard and a plan | kombify Cloud | [Create an account](/apps/cloud/get-started) |
| Ask an AI assistant questions about kombify | AI Guide | [AI Guide and Workbench](/apps/ai/overview) |
| Use kombify from my phone | Companion (internal preview) | [Companion](/apps/companion/overview) |

## Do I need an account?

It depends on the product.

- **StackKits** works without any kombify account. It runs on a computer you
  own and keeps control of your sign-in on that computer.
- **SpeechKit** works without an account. Whether your speech leaves your
  computer depends on the providers you choose; see
  [Choose your privacy settings](/speechkit/privacy-settings).
- **kombify Cloud, the AI Guide's studio and Companion** use the hosted kombify
  sign-in, so they need an account.

## How mature is each product?

Every page states its own status at the top. In short, StackKits and SpeechKit
have public releases (SpeechKit is a beta). Techstack, kombify.me and
Companion are earlier. kombify Cloud is in a public beta. If a page carries a
warning box, read it before you rely on the feature.
