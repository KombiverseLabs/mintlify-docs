Automation is not a use case you can select yet. The team is evaluating a self-hosted workflow engine that connects your other use cases and runs reviewed routines with bounded permissions.

## In plain words

Automation means that the computer does a routine job for you without being asked each time, for example "when this happens, do that". Because Automation is only planned, there is nothing to install, nothing to configure and nothing stored on your host for it yet. This page tells you what the team is looking at so you can decide whether to wait for it.

## What you need first

Nothing for Automation itself. To be ready when it arrives, set up the baseline first: read [Before you start](/guides/stackkits/before-you-start), follow the [Quickstart](/stackkits/quickstart) and learn the routine in [Day to day](/guides/stackkits/daily-use). Routines are most useful once you run at least one use case, such as [Photos](/guides/stackkits/use-cases/photos) or [Media](/guides/stackkits/use-cases/media).

| [Node-RED](https://nodered.org/) | Specialist candidate | Considered for flow-based home and IoT automation. |
| [Windmill](https://www.windmill.dev/) | Specialist candidate | Considered for script- and code-first workflows. |

## What to expect

- A single workflow engine is the starting point, not all four tools at once.
- Recipes will need explicit approval and scoped credentials; automation will not gain access to another use case just because it runs on the same kit.
- Bounded, reviewed starter routines come before an open-ended workflow canvas.

Check the [StackKits changelog](/changelog/overview) or the [use-case catalog](/stackkits/reference/use-case-catalog) for whether Automation has moved out of planning.

## What is stored where

Nothing yet. Once Automation is released, the release notes and the use-case catalog will say where its workflows and credentials are kept. Until then, do not store credentials for it anywhere on the assumption that it exists.

## If something goes wrong

- You cannot find Automation in the selection: that is expected, because it is not released. Check the catalog page above.
- You want a routine job today: keep it manual and review changes with [Review a plan and apply](/guides/stackkits/review-plan-and-apply).
- A service you did install misbehaves: see [Troubleshooting](/guides/stackkits/troubleshooting).
