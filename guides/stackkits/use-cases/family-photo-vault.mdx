A Family Photo Vault is the optional native v2 `photos` workload with Immich on a host you control. The [use-case catalog](/guides/stackkits/use-cases/overview) lists it as a declared component of the published release; the initial StackSpec does not select it.

## Choose the host

- Use [Basement Kit](/stackkits/kits/basement-kit) for local hardware and LAN-first access.
- Use [Cloud Kit](/stackkits/kits/cloud-kit) for an existing VPS or cloud host with a domain you control.

Plan storage and backups before importing a family library. Photos and videos often become the largest data set in a homelab.

## Select the workload

The released contract requires:

- the `photos` workload with the `immich` alternative
- explicit site and node placement
- a supported workload adapter
- an opaque `database-password` secret reference backed by local owner custody

Choose the workload, placement and adapter in your [StackSpec](/guides/stackkits/configure-stack-spec). Its `database-password` slot contains an opaque `secret://` reference; the actual password stays in local owner custody. Follow [Add a workload](/stackkits/reference/day-2-operations#add-a-workload-to-an-existing-workspace) to validate the selection, establish that custody, generate the rollout, inspect its plan, apply and verify.

Recovery needs both the original photos and videos and the Immich database.
Practice a staged restore before relying on the service as the only copy of a family library.

## Keep it maintainable

- Keep the StackSpec and verification output with the workspace.
- Change the StackSpec rather than generated application files.
- Size primary and backup storage for photo and video growth.
- Confirm a staged restore before relying on the service for irreplaceable photos.
- Cover both Immich originals and its database when you plan recovery; either part alone is not a complete restore.
- Create Immich accounts only after the rollout verifies against your own host.

For the application-level steps, see [Immich](/stackkits/apps/immich).
