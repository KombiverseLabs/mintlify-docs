<div className="step-content">



### Enter your photo address.


<p className="step-intro">Open the Immich app. It first asks which server you want to connect to.</p>
<ol className="click-list"><li><span className="click-num">1</span><span>Enter your own Immich address in <span className="key">Server Endpoint URL</span>.</span></li><li><span className="click-num">2</span><span>Tap <span className="key">Next</span> and sign in with your <strong>Immich account</strong>.</span></li></ol>
<div className="micro-note"><span>The address in the picture is only an example. Use the address you open your photo server with in a browser.</span></div>

<div style={{ marginBlock: "1rem" }}>

<p><strong>Where do I get the address and account?</strong></p>


<p>From the person who set up your photo server. If a kombify setup already runs it, open the photo service from your Node Hub. Your Immich account is not automatically your kombify account.</p>

</div>

<div className="step-check"> Next, you see your photo overview.</div>
</div>

<div className="step-content">



### Choose which photos to upload.


<p className="step-intro">For the first test, a small album with one photo you can spare is enough.</p>
<ol className="click-list"><li><span className="click-num">1</span><span>Open the <strong>cloud icon</strong> at the top right. Under <span className="key">Backup Albums</span>, tap <span className="key">Select</span>.</span></li><li><span className="click-num">2</span><span>Select your test album. Choose only the album containing the test photo first.</span></li><li><span className="click-num">3</span><span>Use the back arrow to return to the backup view.</span></li></ol>
<div className="micro-note"><span>Allow the app to access the photos you want. With limited photo access, the app sees only the pictures you shared with it.</span></div>

<div style={{ marginBlock: "1rem" }}>

<p><strong>Include all new photos later?</strong></p>


<p>Select the album where your new pictures land. Check its size before you turn on the upload. A large album takes longer.</p>

</div>

<div className="step-check"> The backup view shows your selected album.</div>
</div>

<div className="step-content">



### Turn on the upload.


<p className="step-intro">Stay on Wi-Fi for the first upload and keep the app open.</p>
<ol className="click-list"><li><span className="click-num">1</span><span>Scroll down in the backup view.</span></li><li><span className="click-num">2</span><span>Turn on <span className="key">Enable Backup</span>. The app uploads the files of your selected album.</span></li><li><span className="click-num">3</span><span>Follow the progress under <span className="key">Remainder</span>, which counts the files still to upload.</span></li></ol>
<div className="micro-note"><span>By default, Immich uploads only on Wi-Fi. The gear icon in the backup settings holds the mobile data options.</span></div>

<div style={{ marginBlock: "1rem" }}>

<p><strong>Upload while the app is closed?</strong></p>


<p>Turn on Background App Refresh for Immich in iOS. iOS decides when the app may run in the background, so an immediate upload is not guaranteed. <a href="#kcg-photos-help">See the steps</a>.</p>

</div>

<div className="step-check"> No uploads are left for your test album.</div>
</div>

<div className="step-content">



### Did your photo really arrive?


<p className="step-intro">Open your Immich address in a browser on a second device and sign in with the same account.</p>
<div className="verify-list"><label><input id="kcg-photos-verify-3-0" data-guide-step="3" data-guide-check="0" type="checkbox"/><span>I can see my test photo in the photo overview.</span></label><label><input id="kcg-photos-verify-3-1" data-guide-step="3" data-guide-check="1" type="checkbox"/><span>The photo opens in the full view.</span></label><label><input id="kcg-photos-verify-3-2" data-guide-step="3" data-guide-check="2" type="checkbox"/><span>The app shows no uploads left for the test album.</span></label></div>

<div className="micro-note"><span>Only tick what you checked yourself. This guide does not read your photo server.</span></div>
<div className="step-check"> Now you can add more albums.</div>
</div>
