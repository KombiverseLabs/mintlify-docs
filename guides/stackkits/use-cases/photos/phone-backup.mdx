</div>

<div className="answer">
Ask the person who set up kombify. Your Immich account belongs to the photo service; it is not automatically your kombify account. <a className="" href="/guides/stackkits/use-cases/photos">Photos setup</a>.

<button className="disclosure-title" type="button" aria-expanded="true"><strong>The address does not open, or I see a certificate warning.</strong></button>
<div className="answer">
Use the public HTTPS address provided for the photo service. It works away from home too. Stop at a certificate warning and ask the person who set up kombify to check the address and certificate; do not bypass the warning.
</div>
</div>

</div>
