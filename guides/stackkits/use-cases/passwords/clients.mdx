<div className="step-content">

### Open your vault in a browser.

<p className="step-intro">Start on a computer. Your vault has its own web address, like a website that only your household uses.</p>

<ol className="click-list">
<li><span className="click-num">1</span><span>Type your vault address into the browser's address bar, for example <strong>https://vault.lab.home</strong>, and press Enter.</span></li>
<li><span className="click-num">2</span><span>Below the login box, click <span className="key">Create account</span>.</span></li>
</ol>
<div className="micro-note">The address in this guide is an example. Use the address you got from the person who set up kombify.</div>

<div style={{ marginBlock: "1rem" }}>
<p><strong>What is a vault address?</strong></p>


It is the web address of your household's password vault. It is the public HTTPS address of the vault, on your own domain or a kombify.me address. It also works away from home. The person who set up kombify can tell you the exact address.

</div>
<div className="step-check">You see the <span className="key">Create account</span> form.</div>

</div>

### Create your account.

<p className="step-intro">Your vault only accepts email addresses that were invited. Use the same address the invitation went to.</p>

<ol className="click-list">
<li><span className="click-num">1</span><span>In <span className="key">Email address</span>, enter your invited email address.</span></li>
<li><span className="click-num">2</span><span>In <span className="key">Name</span>, enter your name. The person who runs the vault, and anyone you share passwords with, can see it.</span></li>
<li><span className="click-num">3</span><span>Click <span className="key">Continue</span>.</span></li>
</ol>

<div style={{ marginBlock: "1rem" }}>
<p><strong>Is this my kombify account?</strong></p>


No. This account belongs only to your password vault. It has its own email address and its own master password.

</div>
<div className="step-check">The page now asks you to <span className="key">Set a strong password</span>.</div>

<div className="step-content">

### Choose your master password.

<p className="step-intro">The master password is the one password you still need to remember. It unlocks all the others.</p>

<ol className="click-list">
<li><span className="click-num">1</span><span>In <span className="key">Master password</span>, enter a long password. A short sentence of four or more words works well.</span></li>
<li><span className="click-num">2</span><span>Enter the same password again in <span className="key">Confirm master password</span>.</span></li>
<li><span className="click-num">3</span><span>Click <span className="key">Create account</span>. If the page suggests the browser extension, click <span className="key">Add it later</span> and then <span className="key">Skip to web app</span>. You connect the extension in step 5.</span></li>
</ol>
<div className="micro-note"><strong>Nobody can recover a forgotten master password</strong>, not even the person who runs your vault. Write it down and keep the note somewhere safe outside the vault.</div>

<div style={{ marginBlock: "1rem" }}>
<p><strong>What does the strength bar mean?</strong></p>


It estimates how hard your password is to guess. Aim for <strong>Strong</strong>. Longer is better than complicated.

</div>
<div className="step-check">Your empty vault opens with the title <span className="key">All vaults</span>.</div>
