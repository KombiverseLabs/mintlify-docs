<GuideSteps id="kcg-passwords-steps" title="Set up your vault step by step." provenance="Screenshots by kombify from a local Vaultwarden install and the official Bitwarden apps · made-up account · English menus." sourcesHref="#kcg-passwords-sources">

<GuideStep tab="Create account">

### Create your vault account.

Start on a computer. Your vault has its own web address, like a website that only your household uses. It only accepts email addresses that were invited.

1. Type your vault address into the browser's address bar, for example **https://vault.home.test**, and press Enter.
2. Below the login box, click <UI>Create account</UI>.
3. Enter the invited address in <UI>Email address</UI> and your name in <UI>Name</UI>.
4. Click <UI>Continue</UI>.

<GuideNote>The address in this guide is an example. Use the address you got from the person who set up kombify.</GuideNote>

<GuideFaq q="What is a vault address? Is this my kombify account?">
The vault address is the web address of your household's password vault. It usually starts with **https://vault.** and ends with your home network's name. The account you create here belongs only to the vault. It has its own email address and its own master password.
</GuideFaq>

<GuideCheck>The page now asks you to <UI>Set a strong password</UI>.</GuideCheck>

<GuideStep tab="Master password">

### Choose your master password.

The master password is the one password you still need to remember. It unlocks all the others.

1. In <UI>Master password</UI>, enter a long password. A short sentence of four or more words works well.
2. Enter the same password again in <UI>Confirm master password</UI>.
3. Click <UI>Create account</UI>. If the page suggests the browser extension, click <UI>Add it later</UI> and then <UI>Skip to web app</UI>. You connect the extension in step 4.

<GuideNote>**Nobody can recover a forgotten master password**, not even the person who runs your vault. Write it down and keep the note somewhere safe outside the vault.</GuideNote>

<GuideFaq q="What does the strength bar mean?">
It estimates how hard your password is to guess. Aim for **Strong**. Longer is better than complicated.
</GuideFaq>

<GuideCheck>Your empty vault opens with the title <UI>All vaults</UI>.</GuideCheck>

<GuideStep tab="First password">

### Save your first password.

Save one login as a test. It shows later that your apps are connected to your vault.

1. At the top right, click <UI>New</UI> and choose <UI>Login</UI>. If a welcome window appears first, click <UI>Skip</UI>.
2. In <UI>Item name</UI>, enter a name you recognize, for example the name of a shop.
3. Enter your <UI>Username</UI>. Next to <UI>Password</UI>, click the circular arrows to create a password, then click <UI>Use this password</UI>.
4. Click <UI>Save</UI>.

<GuideNote>Add the shop's web address under <UI>Website (URI)</UI>. The browser extension then suggests this login on that website.</GuideNote>

<GuideCheck>The message <UI>Item added</UI> appears and your login shows in the list.</GuideCheck>

<GuideStep tab="Browser">

### Connect the browser extension.

The Bitwarden extension first connects to Bitwarden's own servers. Tell it once to use your vault instead.

1. Install the Bitwarden extension from your browser's store and open it from the toolbar.
2. Below <UI>Email address</UI>, open the <UI>Logging in on</UI> menu and choose <UI>self-hosted</UI>.
3. In <UI>Server URL</UI>, enter your vault address with **https://** at the start, then click <UI>Save</UI>.
4. Enter your email address, click <UI>Continue</UI>, and log in with your master password.

<GuideNote>The computer app works the same way. There the menu is called <UI>Accessing</UI>.</GuideNote>

<GuideCheck>The extension shows <UI>Logging in on: vault.home.test</UI> before you log in.</GuideCheck>

</GuideStep>

<GuideStep tab="Phone">

### Connect the phone app.

The phone app needs your vault address once, just like the extension.

1. Open the Bitwarden app and tap <UI>Log in</UI>.
2. Tap <UI>Logging in on</UI> and choose <UI>Self-hosted</UI>.
3. In <UI>Server URL</UI>, enter your vault address with **https://** at the start, then tap <UI>Save</UI>.
4. Enter your email address, tap <UI>Continue</UI>, and log in with your master password.

<GuideNote>The picture shows Android. On an iPhone the screens and labels are the same; Bitwarden's guide shows them: [Bitwarden mobile app](https://bitwarden.com/help/getting-started-mobile/).</GuideNote>

<GuideCheck>The app shows the login you saved in step 3.</GuideCheck>

### Is everything connected?

Check it in the web vault and in one app. Only tick what you checked yourself.

<GuideHelp id="kcg-passwords-help">

<GuideFaq id="kcg-passwords-no-invite" q="I don't have a vault address or an invitation yet.">
Ask the person who set up kombify in your household. They add the Passwords service and invite your email address. [How Passwords works with kombify](/guides/stackkits/use-cases/passwords) · [Owner setup](/stackkits/apps/vaultwarden).
</GuideFaq>

<GuideFaq q="Create account says “Registration not allowed or user already exists”.">
Check that you use exactly the invited email address. If you already created your account, go back and log in instead. Otherwise, ask for a new invitation.
</GuideFaq>

<GuideFaq q="The address does not open, or the browser warns about the connection.">
Try the address on a device in your home network first. If your vault runs only at home, connect to your home Wi-Fi or to the home connection app you were given. Each device must also be set up to trust your home server; the person who set up kombify does this. If the browser says the connection is not private, stop there and ask them. Do not click past the warning. [About home addresses](/guides/stackkits/use-cases/network).
</GuideFaq>

<GuideFaq q="I forgot my master password.">
Nobody can recover it. Your vault is encrypted with it. The person who runs your vault can delete your account so you can start again, but the passwords saved in it are lost. [Bitwarden on master passwords](https://bitwarden.com/help/master-password/).
</GuideFaq>

<GuideFaq q="My Android phone has no Google Play.">
Bitwarden runs its own F-Droid repository. The address is listed on the [Bitwarden download page](https://bitwarden.com/download/).
</GuideFaq>

<GuideFaq q="Why does it say Vaultwarden and Bitwarden?">
Vaultwarden is the program that runs your vault. It is an unofficial server that works with the official Bitwarden apps. Vaultwarden is not associated with Bitwarden, Inc. The pictures show English menus; newer versions and other languages can look different.
</GuideFaq>

</GuideHelp>
