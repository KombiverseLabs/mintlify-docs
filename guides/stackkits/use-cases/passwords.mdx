Passwords gives your household a password vault on a host you operate. Vaultwarden runs the vault, and each person reaches it with a Bitwarden client. The release catalog calls this use case Password Vault and delivers it as the optional `vault` workload. The initial Basement and Cloud StackSpecs do not select it. Passwords is part of the broader [Vault](/guides/stackkits/use-cases/vault) use case, alongside planned service-secret and Bitcoin extensions.

## Before you start

- Decide who gets an account and which registration policy you want.
- Keep recovery material outside the server.
- Confirm that your backups cover the Vaultwarden data volume, and practice a restore before the vault holds secrets you cannot recreate.

## Get the Bitwarden clients

Vaultwarden is compatible with the official Bitwarden clients; you do not need a Vaultwarden-specific app. Install any of the clients from [bitwarden.com/download](https://bitwarden.com/download/): browser extensions, desktop apps for Windows, macOS and Linux, the iOS app from the App Store, the Android app from Google Play, or the Bitwarden CLI.

Point each client at your own server before you sign in:

1. Open the client's settings and find **Self-hosted** or **Region** (browser extension and desktop: the gear icon on the login screen; mobile apps: the settings icon on the login screen).
2. Enter your generated Passwords route as the **Server URL**.
3. Sign in or accept your invitation. Every client you enroll uses the same self-hosted server URL.

## Set it up

Select the workload and complete the owner setup on the [Vaultwarden](/stackkits/apps/vaultwarden) page. The vault is ready for a person once they register in a Bitwarden client, sign in, and create and sync one entry.
