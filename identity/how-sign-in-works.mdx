This page explains, for people who are not technical, who decides who gets in.
The short version: you decide for your homelab, and a hosted sign-in is an
optional extra.

## Two doors

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Your homelab" icon="house">
    You, the owner, control it. The services answer only to people you let in.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Your kombify account" icon="cloud">
    Used for the hosted tools such as kombify Cloud. Sign-in happens on the
    official `login.kombify.io` page.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Having a kombify account never gives anyone access to your homelab, and
deleting the hosted connection never locks you out of it.

## What happens when you open a service at home

1. You open the address of a service.
2. A guard in front of the service, called TinyAuth, sees that you are not
   signed in and sends you to your local identity service, PocketID.
3. PocketID asks you to confirm who you are. It is built around passkeys,
   which means a fingerprint, a face scan or a device PIN instead of a
   password.
4. After you confirm, TinyAuth lets you through to the service.

Some applications have their own sign-in. In that case they use it. TinyAuth is
for services that do not bring a compatible one.

## First-time setup of the owner

Right after the rollout you create the first local owner passkey. Keep recovery
material outside the server and do not share one identity between people. See
[PocketID](/stackkits/apps/pocketid) and [Node Hub](/guides/stackkits/node-hub).

## Rules that always apply

- If the system cannot tell who you are, it says no.
- Access tokens are short-lived and made for one purpose.
- A protected service does not become public when the identity service is
  unavailable.
- An assistant that acts for you can never get more authority than you have.

## Questions

- A person cannot sign in to a service: ask the owner to check that the person
  has a local account. See [Troubleshooting](/guides/stackkits/troubleshooting).
- You cannot sign in to your kombify account: see [Sign-in help](/apps/cloud/sign-in-and-account).
- How kombify handles personal data: [Privacy and data](/trust/privacy).
