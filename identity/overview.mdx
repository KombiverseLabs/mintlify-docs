StackKits keeps the owner of a local homelab authoritative. Installing and
operating a stack does not require a hosted account.

## In plain words

There are two kinds of sign-in. One belongs to your own homelab and stays on
your computer. The other is your optional kombify account. They do not replace
each other.

## What you need first

Nothing from kombify. The local identity comes with the StackKit you install.
A kombify account is only needed if you choose to connect to a hosted service.

## Local owner identity

The released local identity baseline uses:

- **PocketID** as the OpenID Connect identity provider for the homelab owner;
- **TinyAuth** as the forward-auth broker in front of protected applications;
- explicit owner approval for lifecycle operations that change the stack.

Identity data and credentials stay under the local owner's control. Removing
an optional hosted connection must not remove local access or prevent the
standalone lifecycle.

## Optional hosted sign-in

When you choose to connect to a hosted kombify service, interactive sign-in uses
`login.kombify.io`, backed by Auth0 and OAuth 2.0 / OpenID Connect. Public clients
use PKCE with `S256` to complete authorization without an embedded client secret.
Hosted sign-in does not replace or outrank your local homelab identity.

## Hosted permissions and entitlements

The Kombify Gateway validates tokens on the routes it manages, including the
[customer MCP endpoint](/apps/ai/mcp). Authentication identifies the account;
it does not grant every feature or action.

- **Audience:** A token must be valid for the requested audience. Services sharing
  an API audience still enforce the required scopes, permissions, and resource access.
- **Entitlements:** A feature can require account or tenant entitlements in addition
  to a valid sign-in. Customer MCP access, for example, is a separate requirement.
- **Delegation:** A service or assistant acting on your behalf cannot gain more
  authority than you possess. The action's policy determines whether approval is needed.
- **Denied access:** Protected requests with missing, invalid, expired, or insufficient
  authorization are rejected. Follow the returned guidance to sign in again or
  resolve the required account access.

## Local data and account settings

Local identity data and credentials remain in your own environment under your
control. Hosted sign-in uses `login.kombify.io`. Your hosted account and
subscription are managed separately through kombify's account and billing settings.
Hosted sign-in does not move local homelab credentials into those settings.

## Security behavior

- Missing or ambiguous identity information denies access.
- Access tokens expire and are scoped to their intended audience.
- Protected routes do not become public when an identity service is unavailable.
- An assistant acting for a user cannot receive more authority than that user.

## If something goes wrong

- Check the login address. The official domain is `login.kombify.io`.
- If hosted sign-in fails, your local access is not affected. See
  [Sign-in problems and account basics](/apps/cloud/sign-in-and-account).
- To set up or review local sign-in, see [PocketID](/stackkits/apps/pocketid)
  and [TinyAuth](/stackkits/apps/tinyauth).
- Still stuck? Write to [info@kombify.io](mailto:info@kombify.io). Never send
  a password.
