Welcome to the kombify docs. kombify turns homelab and self-hosting into **validated, reusable infrastructure** — pick an architecture pattern, let context handle the details, and deploy.

## Start with StackKits

StackKits are CUE-powered infrastructure blueprints. Basement Kit and Cloud Kit are the installable single-environment kits; Modern Homelab (Preview / Early Access) bridges local and cloud. High Availability ships as an [add-on](https://docs.kombify.io/stackkits/addons/high-availability), not a kit.
