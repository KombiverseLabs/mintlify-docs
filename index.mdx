## Release authority

Release-backed instructions link to the current public artifact rather than to
a frozen version, so a download link here always resolves to the newest
published release. Where a page states a version, that line is generated from
the published release and reconciled automatically. Capabilities in preview are labelled separately and do not receive synthetic download or installation
steps.
