<Update label="v0.6.14" description="2026-10-31" tags={["Stable"]}>

- On desktop, the Overview's app bar now shows icons only, with each name on hover, and the kombify mark rises from the bottom edge, centred under it.
- The analytics banner now opens by itself only on the website. In the app nothing is measured until you choose, and you can decide any time in Settings.
- Household data is now protected in the database itself: a signed-in person can read a household only while they are in it, and removing someone ends their access to it right away.

</Update>
