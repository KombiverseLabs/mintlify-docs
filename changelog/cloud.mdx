{/* release-notes:begin release 0.13.0 2026-11-24 */}
<Update label="v0.13.0" description="2026-11-24" tags={["Stable"]}>

- The homepage opens in 1.5 seconds on every first visit: the heading and both ways in stand right away, without the long opening scene.
- On phones, the website menu is only as tall as its entries, stays inside the floating bar and no longer grows while you scroll.
- The live preview moved from the homepage to the end of the Homelab Builder: place your use cases, then try exactly those running with sample addresses.
- On Cloud Home, the empty home always offers its three ways in and the three-step bar.

</Update>
{/* release-notes:end release 0.13.0 */}

{/* release-notes:begin release 0.6.14 2026-10-31 */}
<Update label="v0.6.14" description="2026-10-31" tags={["Stable"]}>

- On desktop, the Overview's app bar now shows icons only, with each name on hover, and the kombify mark rises from the bottom edge, centred under it.
- The analytics banner now opens by itself only on the website. In the app nothing is measured until you choose, and you can decide any time in Settings.
- Household data is now protected in the database itself: a signed-in person can read a household only while they are in it, and removing someone ends their access to it right away.

</Update>
