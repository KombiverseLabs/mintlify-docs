<Update label="v0.44.4" description="2026-10-30" tags={["Stable"]}>

- Add use cases to the servers you already have: "Add use cases" now appears on the dashboard, the Servers page and each server's page, and lets you pick one of your StackKits or servers before it offers a new server.
- Move freely through the creation wizard: every step you already reached opens from the stepper, the browser's back and forward buttons move between steps, and a reload keeps your place.
- See your applications as they really are: Techstack recognizes far more applications on a server, including containers you started by hand with Docker or Compose, and shows each with its proper name, its logo and an open link that reaches its web interface with the right http or https address.
- Hide or replace any application's logo yourself; the new Credits page lists where the logos come from.
- Devices & people keeps your homelab's owner and the people you planned in the wizard visible when the homelab cannot report sign-in status, and tells you what is missing.
- Techstack for Windows starts again on new installations and on installations created before the last database update.

</Update>
