kombify is one platform with one customer promise: define systems with
StackKits, operate them with Techstack, connect to Kombify Cloud when desired,
and use Kombify AI through governed web, mobile, and voice surfaces.

Use this docs site as the canonical public authority for:

- active product names and boundaries,
- public hosts and routes,
- mode behavior and entitlement expectations,
- customer-facing support and denial behavior.
