Every native kombify product follows the same progressive model.

## 1) Local only

- No kombify account required.
- No mandatory managed dependency.
- Useful on its own for real local/self-host outcomes.

## 2) Connect to Cloud

- Keep local execution while binding identity and account context.
- Enable connected capabilities (monitoring, synchronized status, AI support).
- Preserve explicit offline behavior and local autonomy.

## 3) Managed later

- Follows after lifecycle, economics, rollback, entitlement, and support gates
  are proven.
- Does not replace Local/Connect; it extends them.

<Warning>
Local or self-host evidence is not SaaS proof. Public claims must include
evidence class and environment boundary.
</Warning>
