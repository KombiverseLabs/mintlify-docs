kombify AI has one identity and three policy levels:

1. **Anonymous Guide**: cited public knowledge, no tools, no customer data, no
   durable personal memory.
2. **Authenticated free support**: tenant-aware support with bounded history and
   allowlisted read-only diagnostics.
3. **Paid Operator**: entitlement/quota checked proposals, approvals, execution,
   verification, and durable audit.

## Premium simplicity contract

- One identity and org model.
- One Local only / Connect to Cloud / Managed later model.
- One error/reason/guidance envelope across surfaces.
- Signed updates, rollback path, and explicit side-effect behavior.

## Entitlement safety

Cost-bearing or destructive actions fail closed before side effects.
Denials must include reason and next-step guidance.

<Note>
This page defines product role and policy contract. It is not itself a claim
that every premium surface is currently live in production.
</Note>
