kombify Companion is the phone app that connects your kombify account, your
homelab and your Companion.

<Warning>
Companion is a prototype in internal preview. There is no public store release
yet, and some features below are still being built. Installing it on your phone
is not possible for the public today.
</Warning>

## What it is

- A native app for Android and iOS that connects to kombify Cloud. Sign-in,
  sign-up and account choice happen on the hosted kombify login.
- A Feed that collects items for you in one place, with sections, a lane for
  items that need you, and settings for push notifications.
- Household sharing: send text, links, photos or folders to the people in your
  household.

## What you can do in the preview

In everyday terms, the app is a pocket version of your kombify account. The
parts of it that exist in the preview today:

- **Chat with your Companion.** Write to it, send a file, and read the answer as
  it arrives. Chats are kept so you can continue them. See
  [Chat, voice and decisions in Companion](/apps/companion/chat-voice-and-decisions).
- **Talk instead of typing.** Dictate a message or hold a spoken conversation.
- **Answer decisions that need you.** Actions that change real systems wait for
  your approval.
- **Find your way around.** A setup on the first start lets you pick how you
  want to begin, and a short introduction shows your modules. See
  [Get started with the Companion app](/apps/companion/install-and-sign-in).
- **Android home screen.** Home-screen widgets exist on Android only.

If your phone has no connection, a text message you write is kept and sent when
the connection returns. Not every part has been tried on real phones yet, and
a smartwatch version is still being built.

## What to expect

Feed and household sharing are under development and depend on the matching
Cloud features. They are not generally available. Check the current state of
the app on this page before you plan around them.

Related: [kombify Cloud](/apps/cloud/overview) and
[AI Guide and Workbench](/apps/ai/overview).

## If something goes wrong

- **You cannot find the app in a store.** That is expected. There is no public
  store release yet, and only invited testers get preview versions.
- **A feature described here is missing in your preview.** Parts of the app
  depend on matching features in kombify Cloud and may not be switched on for
  your account yet.
- **Sign-in does not finish.** See
  [Get started with the Companion app](/apps/companion/install-and-sign-in) and
  [Sign-in problems and account basics](/apps/cloud/sign-in-and-account).
- **You want to know what is kept about you.** See
  [What happens to your data in kombify AI](/apps/ai/your-data-and-privacy).
