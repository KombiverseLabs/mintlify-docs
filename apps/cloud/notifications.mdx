kombify can tell you when something needs you, for example a server problem or
a decision that is waiting. You decide what reaches you and where.

## Before you start

Sign in at `app.kombify.io`. Security and account notifications are always on
and cannot be switched off.

## Open the settings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Feed & Push">
    Open **Settings** from the account menu at the bottom of the left rail,
    then choose the **Feed & Push** tab. You can also use the **Feed & Push
    settings** link in the bell menu.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check where kombify can reach you

The **Channels** section lists:

- **Push.** Alerts on the phone or computer where you turned on notifications
  in the kombify app. It is set per device.
- **E-mail.** Goes to your account address, which the page shows. It is used
  for urgent items that are still unread after a while and for the topics you
  switch on.
- **Telegram.** A private chat with the kombify bot that sends a short alert
  with a link back to kombify. You answer in kombify, not in the chat. If the
  page says "Not available yet", it is not switched on for you.

### Link Telegram (when it is offered)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Link Telegram">
    The page prepares a one-time link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Telegram and press Start">
    Choose **Open Telegram**, then press Start in the chat. The link works once
    and shows the time it expires. You can also send the shown command to the
    bot instead.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The page then shows "Linked since" with the date. Choose **Unlink** to remove
the connection, or **New link** if the first one expired.

## Set quiet hours

Quiet hours hold e-mail and Telegram reminders until the window ends.
Security and account alerts are never held. The Feed and push are not affected.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Switch quiet hours on">
    Turn on **Hold e-mail and Telegram reminders**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the times">
    Set **From**, **Until** and your **Time zone**. Start and end must differ.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Choose **Save quiet hours**. The page shows "Saved".
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Choose your topics

Below the channels, the topics are grouped, for example Security & account,
Incidents & system events, Activity & jobs, Summaries & digests, Product news
and Offers & marketing. Marketing offers are off by default. For each topic you
tick the channels you want, such as in-app, e-mail or push. Channels that a
topic requires show "Required — cannot be changed".

Choose **Save preferences**. "Unsaved changes" disappears and "Saved" shows.

## Check that it worked

Reload the page. Your choices are still there. New items appear in the Feed
under the bell.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Your channel settings could not be loaded."** Reload the page.
- **"Check the times and the time zone."** Start and end of the quiet hours are
  the same, or the time zone is missing. Change them and save again.
- **Telegram could not be updated.** Try again in a moment, or choose **New
  link**.
- **You get no e-mails.** Check the address shown under E-mail, your spam
  folder, and that the topic has the e-mail channel ticked.
- **Still stuck.** Write to [info@kombify.io](mailto:info@kombify.io).
