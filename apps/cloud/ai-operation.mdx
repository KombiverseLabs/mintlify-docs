kombify AI can look after a connected server for you. AI operation is in
preview, and the list of actions below can change.

## What you need first

- A server connected to your account. See
  [Connect a server](/apps/cloud/connect-a-server).
- A kombify account. How much AI you can use depends on your plan.

## What kombify AI can read

Reading changes nothing on your server. kombify AI can look at:

- the status and health of your servers and the services on them;
- the processes and containers that run on a server;
- the state of your backup jobs and whether the newest backup is current;
- the updates that are available for the system and for your services.

With this it can tell you why a server looks offline, which service is using
the processor or why a backup failed.

## What kombify AI can change

Each change is a typed action that your homelab runs itself. kombify AI cannot
run a free-form command.

| Action | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Start, stop or restart a service | The service of your StackKit changes state. |
| Update a service | Techstack keeps a restore point, verifies the target live and automatically returns to the previous release when that verification fails. |
| Run or check a backup | A backup runs, or kombify AI reads whether the repository is ready and a verified snapshot exists. |
| Verify the state | Your homelab is checked against its StackKit. |
| Start, stop or restart a container or system service | Applies to ones that your StackKit does not manage. Containers and units that StackKits manages or protects are refused. |

Anything not on this list is not something kombify AI can do on your server.

## How approval works

kombify AI acts with your own rights, never with more. Some actions need your
approval first, and then one click is enough. Every action is checked before it
runs, is logged and leaves a receipt. You can deny an action, and a denied
action has no effect.

## What it uses

AI use draws on the monthly AI credits of your plan. When the credits are used
up, the free models stay available.

## Use your own AI agent instead

You can connect an agent you already use, such as Claude Code, Codex or
Cursor. It runs the local `stackkit` MCP server on the computer that holds your
homelab workspace. By default it can read, and it can change something only
after you turn on a write gate. See
[Connect your own AI agent](/guides/stackkits/connect-your-ai-agent).

## If something goes wrong

- If kombify AI says it cannot reach a server, check the server in Monitoring.
  A server that is offline or limited cannot take an action.
- If an update returned to the previous release, the job result names the
  outcome and the step that failed. Ask kombify AI to read it.
- For anything else, write to [info@kombify.io](mailto:info@kombify.io). Never
  send a password.
