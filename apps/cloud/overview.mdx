kombify Cloud is the hosted account side of kombify. It holds your sign-in,
your plan and the dashboard from which you open the kombify tools you are
entitled to. A homelab can be connected to it, and kombify AI can support or
operate that homelab.

<Warning>
kombify Cloud is in beta. Features can still change, and kombify does not
guarantee availability or offer a service level agreement. Your statutory
rights are not affected. See the terms on kombify.io.
</Warning>

## How the products fit together

- [StackKits](/stackkits/overview) defines your homelab: which kits and apps
  it holds, and how it is installed, backed up and updated.
- [Techstack](/techstack/overview) sets that homelab up and operates it.
- kombify Cloud is the account your homelab can be connected to, with a
  dashboard that shows how it runs.
- [kombify AI](/apps/ai/overview) can support or operate a connected homelab.

## Three ways to run a homelab

These are placements of the same homelab, not tiers. Every placement can be
reached from anywhere through your own domain or a `*.kombify.me` address once
you turn that on, and can also stay on your home network only. Remote reach is
not something only kombify Cloud provides. See [kombify.me](/kombify-me/overview).

| Mode | What it means | kombify account |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Self-managed | You install and run everything on your own hardware with StackKits, or with the Techstack [Windows Beta](/techstack/install-windows). | Not needed |
| Connected | Your own server is bound to kombify Cloud. See [Connect a server](/apps/cloud/connect-a-server). | Needed |
| Managed by kombify | kombify orders and operates a server at a hosting provider. You supply no server and no provider credential. See [Managed servers](/apps/cloud/managed-servers). | Needed |

## What works today

- Sign-up, sign-in and the dashboard at `app.kombify.io`. See
  [Create an account](/apps/cloud/get-started).
- Plans and billing, with monthly or quarterly payment and cancellation at the
  end of a billing period. See [Plans and billing](/apps/cloud/plans-and-billing).
- Your own domain for your stack. See [Connect your own domain](/apps/cloud/domains).
- The Feed and notification choices. See
  [Choose what kombify tells you](/apps/cloud/notifications).
- Households: invite people, choose what each may use, and remove them. See
  [Households](/apps/cloud/household).
- Account deletion. See [Delete your account and your data](/apps/cloud/delete-your-account).
- Self-managed homelabs with StackKits, which work without a kombify account.

## In preview

- Connecting a server, and the Monitoring view that shows its status.
- Managed servers.
- Letting kombify AI operate a connected homelab. See
  [Let kombify AI operate your homelab](/apps/cloud/ai-operation).
- Sign-in on the homelab itself for household members.

## If something goes wrong

Most problems are sign-in problems. Start with
[Sign-in problems and account basics](/apps/cloud/sign-in-and-account). For
anything else, write to [info@kombify.io](mailto:info@kombify.io), say which page
you were on and when it happened, and never send a password.
