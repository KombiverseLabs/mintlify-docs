kombify Cloud is the hosted account side of kombify. It holds your sign-in,
your subscription and the dashboard from which you open the kombify tools you
are entitled to.

<Warning>
kombify Cloud is in public beta. Availability is not guaranteed, and that
includes paid plans. Your statutory rights are not affected. See the beta
section of the terms on kombify.io.
</Warning>

In everyday terms: Cloud is the part of kombify that lives on the internet and
knows who you are. The tools that run on your own computers and servers do not
need it to work, but Cloud makes them easier to reach from one place.

## What Cloud does

- **Account and sign-in.** Sign-in, sign-up and account choice happen on
  kombify's hosted login page, powered by Auth0.
- **Dashboard.** After sign-in you land in the app at `app.kombify.io`, where
  you launch your tools and change settings.
- **Plans and billing.** Plans are billed through Stripe, monthly or
  quarterly, and you can cancel at the end of any billing period.
- **Households.** A household lets people you invite share items with each
  other. Availability follows the beta status above.

## What you can do in the dashboard

- Find your way around the left rail, Home, the Feed and Settings. See
  [A tour of your dashboard](/apps/cloud/dashboard-tour).
- Choose what kombify tells you, and where. See
  [Choose what kombify tells you](/apps/cloud/notifications).
- Connect a domain you own to your stack. See
  [Connect your own domain](/apps/cloud/domains).
- Ask kombify to delete your account and your data. See
  [Delete your account and your data](/apps/cloud/delete-your-account).

## If something goes wrong

Most problems are sign-in problems. Start with
[Sign-in problems and account basics](/apps/cloud/sign-in-and-account). For
anything else, write to [info@kombify.io](mailto:info@kombify.io), say which page
you were on and when it happened, and never send a password.

## What Cloud does not do

Cloud is optional for running your own infrastructure. A kit installed with the
[StackKits CLI](/stackkits/overview) works without a kombify account, and the
[Identity and Access concept](/identity/overview) explains how local and hosted
sign-in relate.
