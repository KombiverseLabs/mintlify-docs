This page shows you where things are in the dashboard at `app.kombify.io`. You
do not need any technical knowledge.

<Warning>
kombify Cloud is in public beta. Some entries may look different for your
account, or may not be shown at all, depending on your plan and on what is
switched on for you.
</Warning>

## Before you start

[Sign in](/apps/cloud/get-started) so that the dashboard opens at
`app.kombify.io`.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Look at the left rail">
    The bar on the left is the rail. It holds the whole navigation. On a phone
    it is hidden behind a menu button at the top; tap the button to open it.
    On a computer you can fold the rail to save space and drag its edge to make
    it wider or narrower.

    You will see these entries:

    - **Home** is your start page.
    - **Monitoring** shows how your homelab runs.
    - **Tools** groups the kombify tools. **Techstack**, **StackKits** and
      **Companion** are there, and **Workpaths** is where you review runs and
      approvals. **Simulate** and **Paperwork** appear only when they are
      available for your account.
    - **Docs** opens this documentation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rest the pointer on a tool">
    On a computer, a small card opens next to a tool entry. It lists that
    tool's own things, for example your connected machines under Techstack or
    your recent sessions under Companion. If a list cannot be read, the card
    says so. That is not the same as having nothing yet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Home">
    Home greets you and shows your homelab: a line that tells you how many apps
    answer and how many machines are online, a search field for your apps and
    use cases, and a card for each use case. If you have none yet, choose **Add
    your first use case**.

    Choose **Arrange** to put the use cases you use most first. The first one
    gets the large card. The order is saved on this device only for now.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the Feed">
    The bell labelled **Feed** in the rail shows what needs you. Open it to
    read items, mark them read or dismiss them, and **Open Feed** shows the
    full list with the views Active, Unread, Needs you, Resolved and Archive.
    To choose what reaches you, see
    [Choose what kombify tells you](/apps/cloud/notifications).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the account menu">
    At the bottom of the rail, your name opens the account menu with
    **Settings**, **Billing**, the display and language controls and **Sign
    out**. Plans and invoices are covered in
    [Change or cancel your plan](/apps/cloud/plans-and-billing).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Look through Settings">
    Settings has these tabs, in this order:

    | Tab | What it is for |
    | --- | --- |
    | Account | Your name and e-mail from your sign-in, replaying the introduction, cookie settings, and the request to delete your account |
    | Stack Identity | The name and character that kombify tools show for your stack |
    | Design | Light or dark, and how the dashboard looks. Changes apply only after you choose **Apply** |
    | Feed & Push | What you are told and where |
    | Keys & connections | Your Wallet and API keys |
    | Dashboard | Which tools your dashboard starts with, and where to get the kombify apps |
    | Budgets & quotas | How much of your plan you have used |
    | Domains | Your domains. See [Connect your own domain](/apps/cloud/domains) |
    | AI assistants | What the kombify AI assistants may do in each product |
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it worked

You know your way around when you can open Home, the Feed and Settings
without searching for them.

## Replay the introduction

In **Settings**, **Account**, the section about getting started has buttons to
replay the setup and the introduction whenever you want.

## If something goes wrong

- **A tool entry is missing.** Some tools show only when they are available for
  your account. Your plan decides what you can use; see
  [Plans and billing](/apps/cloud/plans-and-billing).
- **A hover card says a list could not be read.** Wait a moment and move the
  pointer onto the entry again.
- **You are sent back to the login page.** Your session ended. Sign in again;
  see [Sign-in problems and account basics](/apps/cloud/sign-in-and-account).
- **Still stuck.** Write to [info@kombify.io](mailto:info@kombify.io) and say
  which page you were on and when it happened. Never send a password.
