<Note>
Connected operation is in preview, and the hosted Techstack that carries it can
have limited access. See [Techstack availability](/techstack/availability).
</Note>

Connecting binds a server you own to your kombify account. This is the
Connected mode: the server stays yours, and kombify Cloud shows how it runs.

## In plain words

You sign in, open Techstack from the dashboard, add a server you already have
and run one command on it. When the server reports in, it appears in your
dashboard. You do not need a paid plan for this. An account without a plan can
sign up, run the Techstack setup and bind a server.

## What you need first

- A kombify account. See [Create an account](/apps/cloud/get-started).
- A Linux server with systemd, ideally Ubuntu, and administrator rights on it.
- A way to open a terminal on that server, directly or over SSH.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Techstack">
    Sign in at `kombify.io`, then open **Techstack** under **Tools** in the
    left rail of the dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a Node">
    Start the **Add Node** wizard and choose **I already have a device**. Pick
    **Give me a 1-liner**, or **Connect my device** if you prefer to give
    Techstack an SSH key. See [Add a Node](/techstack/add-node).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the pairing command">
    Copy the command from the progress page and paste it into the terminal of
    your server. It works once and expires after 15 minutes. See
    [Pairing command](/techstack/pairing-command).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the connection">
    The server shows **Not connected yet** until its first report arrives.
    Then it shows as connected, and Techstack continues with the setup of your
    StackKit.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What a connected server gives you in Cloud

- **Status.** Monitoring lists your servers as online, limited or offline,
  with the apps that answer and the disk that is fullest.
- **Backups.** The Backups card shows the last run and whether a copy outside
  your home is set up.
- **Addresses.** It lists what is reachable from the internet and links to
  [your own domain](/apps/cloud/domains).
- **Feed.** Notices about your homelab reach your
  [Feed](/apps/cloud/notifications).
- **Help from kombify AI.** A connected account can ask kombify AI to read and
  diagnose the server. See [Let kombify AI operate your homelab](/apps/cloud/ai-operation).

Cloud keeps no logs. To read the log of a service, open it in Techstack.

## If the connection ends

Your homelab keeps working on its own. When Techstack cannot be reached,
Monitoring says that the status is unknown, which does not mean your apps are
down. The sign-in on the homelab itself is separate from your kombify account,
so removing the connection never removes your local access.

To end the connection yourself, detach the server. Its containers and data are
not touched. See [Revoke, detach and uninstall Guard](/techstack/guard/revoke-and-uninstall).

## If something goes wrong

- The server stays on **Not connected yet**. Check that the server can open the
  Techstack address shown on the progress page, and generate a new command if
  15 minutes have passed.
- The pairing steps are described in [Pair a server with Guard](/techstack/guard/pairing).
- For anything else, write to [info@kombify.io](mailto:info@kombify.io). Never
  send a password or a pairing command.
