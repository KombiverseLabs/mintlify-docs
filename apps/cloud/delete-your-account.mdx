You can ask kombify to delete your account at any time. There is no delete
button that works instantly: you send a request by e-mail, and kombify carries
it out.

## Before you start

- Delete your account only if you are sure. Deleting removes your Stack
  Identity, keys, domains and all your preferences.
- **Save what you need first.** Once the deletion is carried out, your data
  is gone. kombify deletes it without undue delay and at the latest within 30
  days of your request.
- A self-service data export button is not built into the dashboard today. To
  get a copy of your data, ask for it in the same e-mail.
- Running paid plans: deleting your account also cancels them at the earliest
  possible date. kombify points this out to you before deleting. If you only
  want to stop paying, see [Change or cancel your plan](/apps/cloud/plans-and-billing).

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in and open your account settings">
    Open **Settings** from the account menu, then the **Account** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Request deletion">
    At the bottom, in the red "Danger zone", choose **Request deletion**. This
    opens a prepared e-mail to `info@kombify.io` with the subject "Delete my
    kombify account". If no e-mail program opens, write the e-mail yourself
    from the address of your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the e-mail">
    Send it from the e-mail address of your account. You may add a request for
    a copy of your data. To protect your data, kombify may ask you to verify
    your identity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the answer">
    kombify completes the deletion within 30 days.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens to your data

- Your account data is deleted at the latest within 30 days of your request.
- Security and audit logs of your account are kept while the account exists.
  After deletion, personal details in them are deleted or pseudonymised.
- Invoices and accounting records are kept for the periods the law requires.
  The privacy policy lists the periods.

The full text is in the [privacy policy](https://kombify.io/privacy) and the
[terms](https://kombify.io/terms).

## Check that it worked

You receive an answer to your e-mail. After the deletion, signing in with the
account no longer opens your dashboard.

## If something goes wrong

- **The Request deletion button opens nothing.** Write to
  [info@kombify.io](mailto:info@kombify.io) with the subject "Delete my kombify
  account". Never send a password.
- **No answer.** Check your spam folder, then write again and mention the date
  of your first e-mail.
