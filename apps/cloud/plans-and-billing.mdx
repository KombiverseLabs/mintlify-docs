Subscriptions are billed through Stripe. You manage them in the billing portal
that the dashboard opens for you.

A plan decides what your account may use, for example how many managed servers
or hosted websites it includes. In short: Starter is aimed at the homelab you
already run, Pro adds one managed server with daily backups, and All-You-Need
adds two managed servers, three managed services and automated recovery. The
Pricing page of kombify.io is the place to compare the details and the current
prices.

## Before you start

Sign in at `app.kombify.io`. List prices and the plans you can choose are on
the Pricing page of kombify.io. Every paid plan includes kombify Sites and
Paperwork Plus.

## Change your plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open billing">
    In the dashboard, open the account menu at the bottom of the left rail and
    choose the billing item.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a plan and a billing period">
    Compare Starter, Pro and All-You-Need, switch between Monthly and
    Quarterly, then choose a plan. Quarterly costs less than three monthly
    payments. The price is the same in EUR and
    USD. A launch offer, when one applies, is added at checkout.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm in checkout">
    Finish the payment in Stripe's checkout. You return to the dashboard when
    it completes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Cancel

In the billing area, choose **Manage subscription** to open the Stripe billing
portal, then cancel there. The subscription runs until the end of the period
you already paid for.

Timing of other changes: an upgrade applies immediately and is charged pro rata.
A downgrade, a shorter billing period or a cancellation takes effect at the end
of the current period.

## What to expect after each step

- After you open billing, the page lists the plans with the billing period you
  selected and marks your current plan as **Current plan**.
- After you choose a plan, you are sent to Stripe's checkout, a page run by
  Stripe and not by kombify. Enter your payment details there.
- After the payment completes, you return to the dashboard and the billing area
  shows the new plan.

## Check that it worked

The billing area shows your current plan and, after a cancellation, the date
on which it ends.

## If something goes wrong

- **Billing says it is not available for your account yet.** Return to the
  dashboard and try again later.
- **Checkout or the billing portal is temporarily unavailable.** Return to
  Billing and try again in a moment.
- **You are asked to sign in again.** Sign in, then return to Billing.
- **Billing asks you to switch the currency.** Your billing account is billed in one
  currency. Switch the currency on the Billing page to
  the one named in the message and choose your plan again.
- **A promotion code is rejected.** Check the code or continue without it.
- **The billing portal does not open because you have no subscription.** Choose
  a plan first.
- **Still stuck.** Write to [info@kombify.io](mailto:info@kombify.io) and say
  which page you were on and when it happened. Never send a password or card
  details.
