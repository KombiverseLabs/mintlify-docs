This page takes you from nothing to a signed-in dashboard. You do not need any
technical knowledge, and you do not need to install anything: everything
happens in your web browser.

An account is what lets kombify recognise you. It holds your sign-in, your plan
and the settings you choose. Paid plans are covered in [Change or cancel your plan](/apps/cloud/plans-and-billing).

## Before you start

You need an e-mail address you can read. Sign-up is open during the public beta.
A modern web browser that allows cookies and local storage for the site is
enough, because the sign-in uses them.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the sign-up page">
    Go to `kombify.io` and start the sign-up. The page opens the hosted
    login with sign-up selected. Expect the address bar to change to the
    kombify login page, `login.kombify.io`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Enter your e-mail address and choose how you want to sign in on the login
    page. All account steps happen there, not on a kombify form. Follow the
    prompts on that page until it sends you back to kombify.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the dashboard">
    After sign-in you arrive at `app.kombify.io`. Your tools and settings are in
    the dashboard. Expect a start page for your homelab; if you have not set
    anything up yet, it offers to add your first use case.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it worked

You are signed in when the dashboard opens at `app.kombify.io` instead of the
login page.

## If something goes wrong

- **Sign-in fails.** Return to `kombify.io` and start the sign-in again. If the
  problem repeats, use the Support page on kombify.io and name the time of the
  attempt.
- **You are not sure which sign-in you are using.** The kombify account and the
  local sign-in of your own homelab are two different things. See
  [Sign-in problems and account basics](/apps/cloud/sign-in-and-account).
- **The page after sign-in looks different from what you expected.** Some
  entries depend on your plan or on what is switched on for you.
