kombify AI is built around the Companion: one assistant that follows through
on what you ask. You reach it through three places.

<Warning>
Companion Studio and the Workbench desktop client are in Alpha. Features, names and
availability can still change.
</Warning>

## In plain words

kombify AI is one assistant, the Companion, that you can reach in three ways.
You can start with the AI Guide on the public website, where no account is
needed, and move to Companion Studio or the desktop client when you want help
with your own work.

## The places

| Place | Where | Needs an account |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AI Guide | `ai.kombify.io` | No. You can read the product information and ask the AI Guide questions. |
| Companion Studio (web) | `workbench.kombify.io` | Yes. Sign-in uses the hosted kombify login. |
| Workbench (desktop client) | Windows app | Yes. It signs in through the same login. |

## What the AI Guide can see

Questions you ask the AI Guide are sent to the AI service without access to your
account or personal history.

## What you need first

- For the AI Guide: nothing. Open `ai.kombify.io` in your browser.
- For Companion Studio: a kombify account. If you do not have one yet, see
  [Create an account](/apps/cloud/get-started).
- For the Workbench desktop client: the same kombify account.

## What to expect

The AI Guide answers public questions about kombify and treats every question
as a separate turn, so it does not remember the one before. Companion Studio
and the desktop client open the hosted kombify login when you sign in. Because
the products are in Alpha, screens and names may change from one visit to the
next.

## Keys and models

The desktop client holds no provider keys. Which models you can use is decided
by the server side of kombify AI, not by the app on your computer.

## The desktop client

The Workbench desktop client is a Windows-first control surface for local coding
sessions, with optional governed cloud endpoints. It is built on the open source
T3 Code project by T3 Tools, Inc. and credits it. Preview builds are unsigned,
so Windows may warn before you start one.

## If something goes wrong

- If sign-in fails, see
  [Sign-in problems and account basics](/apps/cloud/sign-in-and-account).
- If you are unsure what the Guide or Studio knows about you, read
  [What happens to your data in kombify AI](/apps/ai/your-data-and-privacy).
- For anything else, write to [info@kombify.io](mailto:info@kombify.io). Never
  send a password.
