Companion Studio is the part of kombify AI that you use in your web browser. You
do not install anything for it. You sign in once with your kombify account, and
then you can talk to your Companion.

<Warning>
Companion Studio is in Alpha. Screens, names and what is available to your
account can still change.
</Warning>

## Before you start

You need a kombify account. If you do not have one, create it first with
[Create an account](/apps/cloud/get-started).

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Companion Studio">
    Open `workbench.kombify.io` in your browser. The page is titled kombify
    Companion Studio; "Workbench" names the desktop client. If you are not
    signed in yet, expect a short "Redirecting to sign-in" message: the page
    sends you to the login on its own, so you do not have to find a sign-in
    button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Choose to sign in. The hosted kombify login opens. Sign in or choose your
    account there. Expect the address bar to show the kombify login page while
    you do this.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start working">
    You return to Companion Studio with your account. On your first visit a short
    tour may show where things are; you can skip it. Ask your Companion for what
    you need. [Ask your Companion for help](/apps/ai/ask-your-companion) shows
    what to ask and what to expect back.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it worked

Companion Studio shows your account name instead of the sign-in prompt.

## If something goes wrong

- **You see "Sign-in failed" and a "Try again" button.** Choose "Try again"
  to start the sign-in once more. If it fails again, wait a moment and reload
  the page.
- **The page says it is restoring your session.** Wait a few seconds. If it does
  not go away, reload the page.
- **You do not have an account yet.** Create one first with
  [Create an account](/apps/cloud/get-started).
- **You are not sure which sign-in is which.** The kombify account and the local
  sign-in of your own homelab are two different things. See
  [Sign-in problems and account basics](/apps/cloud/sign-in-and-account).
- **You want to leave.** Open the user menu and choose **Sign out**.
- **Still stuck.** Write to [info@kombify.io](mailto:info@kombify.io), say which
  page you were on and when it happened, and never send a password.

## Cookies and local storage

The Workbench uses essential cookies or local storage for sign-in, security,
theme, language and session handling. It works without any others.
