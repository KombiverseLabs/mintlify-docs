## Goal

A visitor asks what kombify does and gets a source-cited path to:

- docs (`docs.kombify.io`),
- OSS entry (StackKits/Techstack),
- pricing/commercial entry,
- or signup flow.

## Boundaries

- Tool-free Guide only.
- No customer data.
- No account-scoped actions.
