## Goal

A paid user:

1. completes checkout and receives entitlement/quota,
2. receives a real proposal/action card,
3. denies one request and approves another,
4. gets execution plus verification evidence.

## Boundaries

- Side effects are blocked until entitlement and policy checks pass.
- Denials must preserve reason and guidance envelopes.
